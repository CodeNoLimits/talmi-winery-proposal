No risk

Advertising wine, by the book.

In Israel, the 2012 law restricting alcohol advertising limits what an ad may show and requires a warning. So the paid ads sell the place: the view, the cheese, the baskets, the happenings. The wine appears in organic posts and videos with the legal Hebrew warning, and ads are shown to people aged 18 and over only. That is also what gets them approved by Meta.
